# Phase 15 Assurance — CodeRabbit-style Review Export

Phase 15 exports canonical review runs to `canonical`, `github`, `gitlab`, `cli`, and `json` profiles. The canonical review bundle includes summary, walkthrough, changed-files table, sequence-diagram placeholder or evidenced cross-repository sequence, impact reports, findings, inline comments, suggested-fix guidance, coverage, traceability, and merge gate. Exporters preserve canonical findings and evidence and validate source-manifest and output checksums.
