# Cheap-Model Compatibility Contract

Economical models are a supported execution baseline, not a degraded best-effort mode.

The architecture compensates for limited reasoning capacity through:

- one objective per invocation;
- small evidence slices;
- explicit definitions and stop conditions;
- machine-readable schemas;
- deterministic routing and stable templates;
- sequential default execution;
- fresh independent critique;
- deterministic semantic verification;
- explicit `UNKNOWN`, `CONFLICT`, and loopback outcomes;
- no hidden requirement for a named flagship model.

A stronger model may improve speed or synthesis quality but may not bypass evidence, authority, freshness, critique, or verification contracts.
